Global Modifiers

Building-speed sources (Title, Construction Aid, VIP, Lifetime Pass, Monthly Pass) sum additively into one total %, then a single time factor is applied — same model as the Tech calculator. Development maxed, Builder Class Buff and Master Builder all discount resource cost and stack additively. Building Helper reductions are flat and applied per individual level-up (not per total), after the speed factor, floored at 0.
